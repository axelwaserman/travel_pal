# Messaging

> Executes [[positioning]] against [[personas]] and the tiers in [[tier-matrix]]. Working name **Glasswing** ([[naming]]). All claims honest & probabilistic ([[positioning]] guardrails); tiers referenced **by name only — prices are owned by [[sales]]**.

## Message architecture

- **Category:** transparent flight-reliability engine (not "another delay tracker").
- **Core promise:** *Know which flight to trust — and see exactly why.*
- **Proof pillars:** (1) **Transparent** — published, backtested method; (2) **Route-shopping** — compare carriers/times, not just track one flight; (3) **Free to look** — the analytics cost you nothing.
- **Never:** accuracy superiority, guarantees, invented stats.

## Value props by persona

| Persona ([[personas]]) | Their pain (real user language, [[demand-evidence]]) | Glasswing value prop | Tier fit ([[tier-matrix]]) |
|------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|----------------------|----------------------------|
| **P1 Optimizing Nomad** | *"Airlines know your flight will be delayed long before they tell you."* | Shop routes by real reliability; get an explained ≥3–6h heads-up so you rebook before the queue forms | **Plus** → **Pro** |
| **P2 Anxious Occasional** | *"Just tell me plainly if I should worry — and what to do."* | A clear, honest read with an uncertainty band, in plain language — free | **Free** (→ insurance/rebook handoff) |
| **P3 Corporate Travel / TMC** | 80% of business travelers hit disruption; duty-of-care exposure | Steer bookings to structurally reliable routes; an auditable risk feed you can defend to stakeholders | **Team / API** |
| **P4 Parametric Insurer / Fintech** | Need calibrated, defensible delay risk to price & trigger payouts | Backtested, calibration-metadata'd `P(delay ≥ Xh)` feed — cheap and auditable, not a black box | **Team / API** |

## Value props by tier (benefit-led, no prices)

- **Free** — *"See any route's real track record — free, forever."* Transparent route/carrier reliability + uncertainty bands. The acquisition wedge.
- **Plus** — *"Get told before the airline tells you."* Proactive ≥3–6h delay alerts on your watched flights + full route analytics.
- **Pro** — *"Foresight for people who live in airports."* Unlimited watchlist, full history, multi-trip risk.
- **Team / API** — *"A flight-risk feed you can audit — and afford."* Calibrated per-flight feed with Brier/reliability metadata, SLA, portfolio scoring.

## Landing page — hero

**Headline:**
> **Know which flight to trust.**

**Subhead:**
> Glasswing reads a decade of on-time data and live conditions to tell you how reliable a route really is — with the method shown, the odds honest, and the analytics free.

**Three supporting benefit lines:**
1. **Shop the route, not just the flight.** Compare carriers and departure times by real reliability before you book.
2. **No black box.** Every forecast is calibrated, backtested, and explained — see exactly why we flagged it.
3. **Told before the airline tells you.** Proactive heads-up on at-risk flights, in time to actually rebook.

**Primary CTA:** `Check a route — free` → **Secondary CTA:** `Get delay alerts` (→ Plus) · `For teams & insurers` (→ Team/API)

### Honesty microcopy (ship alongside any forecast)
- *"An estimate, not a promise — here's our confidence band and how we got it."*
- *"Based on historical performance + current conditions. Airlines make the final call."*

## B2B one-liner (LinkedIn/X, P3/P4)

> *"An auditable flight-delay risk feed — calibrated, backtested, and priced for mid-market, not just enterprises."*

## Tone rules

- Plain over clever; specific over hype. A number always comes with its band and its reason.
- Confident about *transparency & cost*, humble about *certainty*.

## Handoff

- → [[brand-system]]: headline/tone feed the visual + voice system.
- → [[lead-gen-plan]]: hero + alt headlines are the A/B test inputs; benefit lines seed ad/SEO copy.
- → [[staff-ml-engineer]]: confirm the public calibration/backtest claims are defensible before they ship.
